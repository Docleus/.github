# Code of conduct

Docleus welcomes contributors of every background and experience level. Treat
people with respect in issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project spaces.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and code constructively; give specific, actionable feedback.
- Assume good intent, listen to corrections, and make room for other people.
- Respect names, pronouns, boundaries, and the privacy of contributors.
- Help keep discussions focused, accessible, and welcoming to newcomers.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, sexual attention, personal
attacks, and sharing private information without consent are unacceptable.
The same standards apply when representing the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

Ask a maintainer for a private contact channel without posting names, incident
details, or private information in a public issue. Send the report only after
that channel is established. Include relevant links or context and any immediate
concerns. Do not use a public issue to investigate or publicize a report.

Maintainers handle reports discreetly and share information only as needed to
address the incident. A maintainer involved in a report must step aside from
reviewing it; another maintainer handles the report when one is available.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, ask participants to change their
behavior, issue a warning, or limit or end participation. The response should
reflect the severity, impact, and recurrence of the behavior. When practical,
explain the decision and the behavior expected going forward in private.
